Things I enjoy

My hobbies & interests

I enjoy drawing and sketching portraits, places, and ideas on paper and digitally, because it allows me to express my creativity, myself, my thoughts, and capture every moment using my own hands.

Drawing portraits

I love drawing portraits because it allows me to express the way I see and admire people I know and I like.

Pencil sketching

Sketching with pencil helps me practice lines, shading, and small details. And also it gives a unique and wonderful feeling when I create something I'm proud of.

Drawing places

I enjoy sketching scenes and adding color to buildings, trees, and the space around them because it can distract me from daily stress and sadness.

Digital art

Creating art digitally lets me experiment to practice different techniques and create unique compositions.

Little things that make an art day

  • Drawing portraits: practicing faces, expressions, and details.
  • Trying different styles: using pencil, color, or digital tools.
  • Relaxing with art: sketching during a quiet break after a long day.

Each drawing gives me a chance to improve and try something new. I want to pursue my passion for art and continue learning and growing as an artist, my current financial stability won't allow me.
